How each derived figure is calculated

Nothing on these pages is stored in the extract — every figure below is computed from it at load, so a new extract restates all of them.
MeasureDefinitionReads as a problem when
Reporting period The period containing today’s date, found by fitting the extract’s own finish dates against its period numbering. Overridable in the sidebar. n/a — it sets the cut-off every other measure uses
Achieved rate Activities recorded complete in the six closed periods before the reporting period, divided by six. The period in progress is excluded because it is still filling. It falls period on period
Required rate Outstanding activities ÷ periods from the reporting period to the last planned finish period, inclusive. It rises while the achieved rate does not
Delivery pressure Required rate ÷ achieved rate. A multiple of the pace the programme has actually demonstrated. Above 1.0 — watch; above 2.0 — the plan needs re-basing or resourcing
Projected finish / slip Outstanding ÷ achieved rate, counted forward from the reporting period, then compared with the last planned period. Slip is positive
Sign-off coverage RAB count ÷ sign-off count for the same group. Every red line as-built is expected to carry an associated sign-off. Below 100%, unless the group only reviews sign-offs by role
Gap Sign-off count − RAB count. Positive is a red-line shortfall; negative is red-line ahead. One group’s gap approaches or exceeds the programme net gap
Past due Activities with a planned finish period earlier than the reporting period that are not recorded complete. Above zero
Period load ratio Outstanding activities planned into one period ÷ the achieved rate — how many periods of demonstrated throughput that single period asks for. Above 1.5 — watch; above 3.0 — not deliverable at today’s pace

Sign-off coverage gap

Every red line as-built is expected to carry an associated design sign-off. Bars to the right are a RAB shortfall against sign-off; bars to the left are RAB ahead of sign-off.
Gap = sign-off count − RAB count. Coverage = RAB ÷ sign-off. A team that only reviews sign-offs, or only draws red lines, shows an extreme coverage figure by role rather than by omission — read the two counts alongside the ratio.
