Services

Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, done properly

Six services, one specialism. Whether you need a single stubborn dashboard fixed or a full migration to Fabric, everything below draws on the same Microsoft data stack. Most projects use two or three of them.

PBI · 01

Power BI Consulting & Development

Most Power BI problems are not really Power BI problems. They are data modelling problems wearing a dashboard. I fix the model first, and the report tends to follow.

Talk to us about Power BI
  • Star schema data modelling and DAX measure design
  • Report and dashboard design, from executive summaries to operational detail
  • Power BI Service administration: workspaces, capacities, deployment pipelines
  • Row level security and access governance
  • Embedded analytics for internal tools or customer facing apps
  • Performance tuning for slow reports and oversized models
FAB · 02

Microsoft Fabric Implementation

Fabric replaces a stack of separate tools with one platform. I help you move across without disrupting the reporting people rely on today.

Talk to us about Fabric
  • Lakehouse and OneLake architecture design
  • Data Factory pipelines for ingestion and transformation
  • Migration from legacy warehouses or standalone ETL tools
  • Real time analytics with Eventstream and KQL
  • Capacity planning and cost management
  • Integration with existing Power BI content
BIS · 03

Data & BI Strategy

Before anyone builds anything, it helps to agree on what good reporting actually means for your business. That conversation saves more time than any tool choice.

Talk to us about strategy
  • Data and reporting audits
  • KPI and metrics framework design
  • Governance models: who owns what data, and who can change it
  • Tool and licensing recommendations across Power BI and Fabric capacity tiers
  • Self service BI rollout planning
RPT · 04

Reporting Modernization

If your reporting system is really a folder of spreadsheets that one person maintains, this is where we start.

Talk to us about reporting
  • Auditing existing manual and Excel based reports
  • Rebuilding priority reports as governed Power BI datasets
  • Automating recurring reports and distribution
  • Executive dashboard design
  • Change management and training for report consumers
ETL · 05

Data Warehousing & Pipelines

Reports are only ever as reliable as the data feeding them. I build the plumbing so it holds up when it matters.

Talk to us about pipelines
  • Data pipeline design and orchestration
  • Warehouse and lakehouse architecture
  • Source system integration across ERP, CRM, finance systems, and others
  • Data quality checks and monitoring
  • Documentation of data lineage
EDU · 06

Training & Enablement

I would rather teach your team to fish. Most engagements end with a training plan rather than an open ended support contract.

Talk to us about training
  • Hands on Power BI workshops for report builders and analysts
  • Microsoft Fabric onboarding for data teams
  • DAX and data modelling deep dives
  • Internal documentation and style guides
  • Office hours style support during the handover period
